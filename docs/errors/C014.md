# C014 — Cap-typed actor-state field assigned a capability without a recognised full-authority origin

- **Category:** Capability
- **doc_url:** `sigil://errors/C014`

Every read of a cap-typed actor-state field counts as FULL authority — in the slot escape gate (C013), in the Z3 prover and in the Lean verifier — so the field may only be assigned a capability whose origin is one those checkers can trust: a non-closure parameter, `mint`, an actor-state read or a direct call result, directly or through `let`, `draw` or `split`. A `.restrict` result, a `slot_take` result, a closure-call or FFI result, or a field load may not be stored in state, in `init` or anywhere else. Assign the bare `init` parameter (`fuel = f;`) and call `.restrict(...)` on the value read from the field at the point of use; keep a narrowed capability in a local, never in state.
