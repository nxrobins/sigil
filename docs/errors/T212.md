# T212 — Refinement predicate references a non-i64 field

- **Category:** TypeCheck
- **doc_url:** `sigil://errors/T212`

Refinement predicates may only constrain `i64`-typed fields in Wall 4 Step 1. Cap-typed fields, aggregate-typed fields, and other primitive types are out of scope; Wall 2's deadline-typed caps already cover cap refinement. Move the refinement off the non-i64 field, or expose an `i64` projection that carries the constraint.
