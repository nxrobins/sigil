# T219 — Cross-field refinement RHS field is not `Type::I64`

- **Category:** TypeCheck
- **doc_url:** `sigil://errors/T219`

Wall 4 Step 4 admits cross-field refinements only when BOTH the LHS field AND the RHS field are `Type::I64` (per V60 / anti-goal A9). T212 covers non-i64 LHS; T219 covers non-i64 RHS. Cap-typed fields (A10), other integer types (i32, u32, u64), bool, and named-record fields are out of scope. Convert the RHS field to `i64`, or drop the cross-field clause.
