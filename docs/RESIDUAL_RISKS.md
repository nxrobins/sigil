# SIGIL residual-risk register

This register names soundness and evidence risks that remain after applying the boundary in
[`SECURITY_MODEL.md`](SECURITY_MODEL.md). `Open` means the completion gate is not yet satisfied.
`Accepted` is reserved for a deliberate product boundary with an owner, review point, and live
tracking issue. The completion gate permits only `Closed` and bounded `Accepted` rows. The
unproven-claim census in [`CLAIMS.md`](CLAIMS.md) is the detailed source inventory; grouped risks
here must not hide or delete its rows.

## Severity rule

- **Critical:** a demonstrated path to accepting a violating artifact or escaping runtime isolation.
- **High:** a production enforcement gap, fail-open path, or unguarded deletion/drift path.
- **Medium:** a bounded implementation/evidence mismatch that does not itself authorize execution.
- **Low:** documentation, provenance, precision, or maintenance risk inside an explicit boundary.

No risk may remain `Open` at the completion gate. A non-closed risk must be deliberately bounded,
`Accepted`, assigned to an owner and review point, and linked to a live tracking issue.
